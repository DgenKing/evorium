Henry Street, Ancoats

Real streets.
Every story.

Blossom, Hood, Jersey and Murray Streets meet Henry Street, where a corner pub that’s been pulling pints for a hundred years stands beside the newsagent two doors down. The Crooked Billet and Patel’s News are fictional businesses at the heart of a fight for the street’s future.

Meet the APCs

They don’t exist
to serve you.

They’re not scripted NPCs. They’re APCs: AI Player Characters, with lives, memories and ambitions of their own. Twenty neighbours, each with a personality, a past, money worries, secrets and grudges. They talk to each other when you’re not there, make plans, keep promises, break them, and remember who was kind and who wasn’t. You’re simply another person in their world.

Your reputation is the game

Be decent. Or don’t.
They’ll remember
either way.

There are no quest markers telling you who to trust. Help someone and word spreads. Cross someone and doors close: the pub bars you, nobody lends you a penny, and the gossip follows you down the street. Your standing with the neighbourhood is everything.

Win them over

Tips, favours, a seat at the bar, a friend when you need one.

Cross them

Barred, ignored, talked about, on your own.

You won’t always get a second chance.

How it works

Real people,
not scripts.

Ordinary lives. Extraordinary possibilities.

  1. They think for themselves.

    Every line is written in the moment, in character, by AI.

  2. They remember what matters.

    Promises, insults, kindness, secrets: kept, scored, never forgotten. And their word gets checked: say “done” without doing it and the street finds out.

  3. They make real choices.

    Sign the deal, raise the prices, warn the neighbours. Choices with consequences.

  4. The rules keep it real.

    Money, time and opening hours are enforced by the game, so nobody can magic their way out.
