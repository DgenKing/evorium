Welcome to Evorium

A living street in Ancoats, Manchester, where every person is an APC: an AI Player Character. They have jobs, money worries, secrets, friendships and grudges. They talk to each other, make plans, keep and break promises, and remember how you treated them. The world runs on the real clock, around the clock, whether anyone is watching or not.

You're Thomas. You log in with your own name and password, and you have your own character. The APCs get to know you separately from Rob (the owner's character): your own conversations, reputation, promises and "While you were away". Be decent, or don't, but they'll remember either way.

You can see everything ("god view")

You have the same view as the owner. That means you can see things the APCs themselves keep private:

  • Their private thinking: the small grey "why:" line under each thing an APC says is what they were thinking, not what they said out loud.
  • Their secrets and money: who knows what, Dez's savings, the offers and deadlines on the terrace.
  • The controls that run the whole world: the clock, auto mode, the director and Restart server.

The APCs don't know you can see all this. When you talk to them, they only know what you've actually said to them and what they've heard from others.

The clock speed: please read this bit

Always leave the clock on "1× real time". Never leave it running fast. Every conversation an APC has costs real money (AI calls on the owner's account). Speeding the clock up makes the whole day happen faster, so conversations happen faster, and the credit goes much faster.

The Clock drop-down on the dashboard sets how fast time passes in Evorium:

SettingWhat it meansCost
1× real timeEvorium's clock is the real UK time. A minute is a minute. This is where it lives.Normal: roughly 10–20 cents a day for the whole world
10×Ten minutes pass every real minuteAbout 10 times faster
60×An hour passes every real minuteAbout 60 times faster
600×Ten hours pass every real minuteBurns through credit very quickly

Faster speeds are only for a quick test ("what happens tonight?"), and only for a minute or two. Then put it straight back to 1× real time. Choosing 1× also snaps the clock back to the real time, so the world lines up with the real day again. If you ever find it running fast, set it back to 1×.

Getting around

The ☰ menu in the top-right corner of every page takes you anywhere:

  • Dashboard: the control room: everyone's cards, the live feed, the controls.
  • Map: a 2D view of the street with people walking about.
  • 3D: walk round Shuttle Street yourself.
  • Timeline: everything that's ever happened.
  • Guide: this page.

The EVORIUM logo always takes you back to the dashboard.

The dashboard

Across the top

  • The date and time in Evorium (the same as real UK time when the clock is on 1×).
  • The version (small grey code, e.g. v 32b7b5a): which update the server is running.
  • Restart server: restarts the game. Don't press it unless the owner asks you to.

The controls row

  • Run conversation: makes two people who are in the same place talk right now (pick who, or leave "random", and how many lines). Each one costs a little, so use it now and then, not over and over.
  • Let them live / Stop: auto mode. When it's on, the world runs itself: APCs chat every 20 in-game minutes or so, make their morning plans and react to news. Leave it the way you found it (normally on).
  • Clock: see above. Keep it on 1× real time.
  • Director: a quiet helper that nudges the story with a small everyday event if things get stuck. Leave it ticked.

The left-hand column

  • The terrace: the big story. Northgate Estates owns the pub, the shop and the empty unit, and wants Dez's rent up from £2,400 to £3,900 a month and Raj out for £45,000. It shows the deadlines, the pint price, the quiz, Jess's shifts and the pub's money (private figures are labelled with who knows them).
  • One card per APC: their mood, where they are now, what they want (their goal), today's plans, what they're thinking about doing, their relationships with everyone, promises, and what they've recently learned. Click a card to open it fully.

The middle

  • Where everyone is: who's in the pub, the shop and on the street right now.
  • You: your own character: your name and a line about you, buttons to walk into the pub, the shop or leave, the box to say something, Your promises and What you've learned.
  • The latest conversation: every line, with each APC's private thinking under it.
  • Live feed: what the server is doing right now, line by line.
  • While you were away: when you come back after a while, a summary of what you missed and what people have been saying about you.

At the bottom

API calls, tokens and spend: how many AI calls have been made and roughly what they've cost. If you see it climbing quickly, check the clock is on 1×.

Talking to APCs

  1. Go where they are: walk into the Crooked Billet or Patel's News, or find them on Shuttle Street. You can only talk to someone who's actually there.
  2. Type in Say something and press Enter (or Send). They answer in character, from what they know.
  3. When you've finished, press End conversation (or just walk away). The game then writes up what happened: what they learned, how they feel about you now, and any promises.
They remember. If you promise something, it gets a deadline. Keep it and they trust you more; break it and word gets round. Insult someone and the whole street may hear about it.

The map

The ⚙ button has the settings: quality (use "low" if it's slow), name tags and street life (the cat, dog, fox, pigeons and blowing rubbish). You'll see Rob and any other players walking about by name too.

The timeline

The permanent record of everything: conversations (as summaries), promises made and kept or broken, people arriving, offers, decisions and the daily digest. Filter it by person, by "important only", or by kind.

Do's and don'ts

  • Do leave the clock on 1× real time.
  • Do leave "Let them live" and the Director as you found them.
  • Don't press Restart server unless the owner asks.
  • Don't spam "Run conversation": the world makes its own conversations, and each costs a little.
  • Do play however you like. The APCs will treat you the way you treat them.

Anything odd, or something looks broken? Tell the owner.
